NASA archive photographs and observational images

The 20 nasa-* image presets were retrieved from the NASA Image and Video Library
on 2026-10-13. Individual source IDs, URLs, credits, dates, processing descriptions,
rights references, and asset hashes are recorded in catalog.json and NOTICE.txt.

Usage guidance:
https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/jpl-image-use-policy/

These images are used as an attributed image collection under the NASA media
guidelines. They are not relicensed under Tano's MIT license or the CC0 text
bundled for the museum images. Preserve each image's complete credit when reusing
it. NASA/JPL and the credited contributors do not endorse Tano or its users.
NASA branding, third-party copyrighted material, and identifiable persons have
separate restrictions; the linked policies govern subsequent uses.

Tano resizes and WebP-encodes the selected photographs and telescope/spacecraft
observations without additional color changes or generated content. Composites,
false-color observations, and panoramas retain the source processing and are
identified in the catalog and display titles. The cover viewport may crop them.
