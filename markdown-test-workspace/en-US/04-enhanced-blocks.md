## Five callout types

> [!NOTE]
> A regular note with **emphasis**.

> [!TIP]
> A suggested action.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Important information.

> [!WARNING]
> Warning information.

> [!CAUTION]
> Proceed with caution.
